## Abstract note

In this work, I implement a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) to solve the 1D Burgers' equation. I begin with a simple feed-forward neural network (FFNN), which is trained using the finite-difference solution of the Burgers' equation. I then extend this FFNN into a PINN that learns the governing equation directly by minimizing the residuals of the PDE. I perform parametric evaluations for both the FFNN and PINN models and compare their computational performance. To improve results for simulations with long run times, where the standard PINN struggles, I introduce and apply a Time-Marched PINN approach. Finally, I assess the effectiveness of the method and suggest a few possible directions for improvement. This write-up serves as a practical companion for those beginning their journey with PINNs, particularly readers interested in computational trade-offs, training challenges, and implementation strategies using simple 1D PDEs like the Burgers' equation.
