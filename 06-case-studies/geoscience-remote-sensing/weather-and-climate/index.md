# Weather and Climate AI Specialty Track

Main knowledge page: [Weather & Climate AI](../../../01-knowledge-base/08-weather-climate-ai/index.md).

## 1. Learn the whole forecasting system

```text
observations
→ QC
→ data assimilation
→ initial state / analysis
→ deterministic or ensemble forecast
→ post-processing / downscaling
→ verification
```

AI can replace or augment each block.

## 2. Method map

### Forecast backbones
Graph/mesh networks, Transformer-style models, neural/spectral operators and hybrid numerical-ML systems.

### Probabilistic weather
Ensembles, diffusion/score-based generation, calibrated event probabilities.

### Nowcasting
Radar/satellite sequence prediction for precipitation/cloud/storm evolution.

### Downscaling
Statistical/deterministic/generative mapping from coarse forecasts to local high-resolution fields.

### Data assimilation
Observation-to-state estimation and learned/hybrid assimilation.

### Climate
Long-range statistical structure, coupled components, emulation and physically meaningful distributional evaluation.

## 3. Evaluation

Track variable, pressure level, grid, lead time and verification source. Use deterministic and probabilistic metrics appropriately, then stratify extremes and physical consistency.

## 5. Connection to remote sensing and carbon

Weather is not an isolated domain: meteorological forcing drives ecological fluxes, satellite/radar observations feed state estimation, and land-surface processes couple water, energy and carbon. This makes weather/climate AI a central bridge between EO and terrestrial carbon modeling.
