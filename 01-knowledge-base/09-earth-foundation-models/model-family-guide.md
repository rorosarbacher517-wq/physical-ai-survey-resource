# Earth Foundation Model Family Guide · 截至 2026-08-20

## 1. Prithvi-EO-2.0

**Interface:** downloadable ViT/MAE encoder weights。

Official repo confirms：
- 4.2M global HLS time-series samples；
- HLS 30 m；
- 300M / 600M variants；
- time/location embedding variants。

Use case：fine-tune / extract EO features。

Source: https://github.com/NASA-IMPACT/Prithvi-EO-2.0

---

## 2. TerraMind

**Interface:** multimodal generative EO model + weights/code。

ICCV 2025：
- nine geospatial modalities；
- dual token-level + pixel-level representation；
- any-to-any generative formulation。

Source: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Jakubik_TerraMind_Large-Scale_Generative_Multimodality_for_Earth_Observation_ICCV_2025_paper.html

---

## 3. AlphaEarth Foundations

**Interface:** model + ready-made annual global Satellite Embedding dataset。

截至 cutoff：
- annual 2017–2025；
- 64-dimensional embedding；
- current public collection generated by model v2.1；
- Earth Engine/GCS distribution。

Official: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_SATELLITE_EMBEDDING_V1_ANNUAL

---

## 4. TESSERA

**Interface:** pixel-wise EO embeddings + model/research stack。

v1：global annual representation route。

`TESSERA v2`：2026-07 preprint，研究 scaling、distillation 和 Matryoshka embeddings；planned v2 global embeddings 不应在实际发布前描述为已完成数据产品。

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03949

---

## 5. MaRS

**Interface:** VHR SAR–optical multimodal pretrained backbone。

AAAI 2026：
- cross-granularity contrastive learning；
- meta-modality attention；
- VHR SAR-optical setting。

Source: https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i14.38153

---

## 6. Aurora

**Interface:** Earth-system dynamical FM + task adaptation。

Nature 2025：pretrained on >1M hours diverse geophysical data，适配 weather/air quality/waves 等。

2026 `Aurora 1.5` 增加 open weather-oriented capabilities。

Sources:
- https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/aurora-a-foundation-model-for-the-earth-system/
- https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/aurora-1-5-extending-open-foundation-models-for-weather-and-earth-system-applications/
